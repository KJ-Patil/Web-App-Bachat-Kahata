## 🚀 Suggested New Features (Based on 2026 Trends)

### 🔥 High Impact — Easy to Add

| # | Feature | Why It's Trending | Effort |
|---|---------|-------------------|--------|
| 1 | **Subscription Tracker** — Detect recurring payments, show annual cost, highlight unused subscriptions | Users lose ₹1000s/year on forgotten subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify, gym). Very popular in all top finance apps. | Medium |
| 2 | **"What-If" Simulator** — "If I save ₹5,000/month for 3 years at 8% interest, what do I get?" | Interactive compound interest / future value calculator. Highly engaging. | Easy |
| 3 | **Spending Streaks & Badges** — "7 days under budget! 🔥" achievement system | Gamification drives habit formation. Top finance apps like Cleo, Fortune City use this heavily. | Easy |
| 4 | **Safe-to-Spend Card** — Show one single number: "You can safely spend ₹X today" after bills & savings are deducted | The "anti-budget" approach. Users love simplicity over complex budget tables. | Easy |
| 5 | **Receipt Scanner (OCR)** — Snap a photo of a receipt, auto-extract amount and category | Massive time saver. Trending in all modern expense trackers. | Medium |

### 💎 High Impact — Medium Effort

| # | Feature | Why It's Trending | Effort |
|---|---------|-------------------|--------|
| 6 | **Predictive Spending Alerts** — "At this pace, you'll exceed your Groceries budget by June 25th" | AI-driven proactive warnings instead of reactive "you exceeded!" alerts. | Medium |
| 7 | **Net Worth Dashboard** — Single view: Assets (savings, investments, property) minus Liabilities (loans, EMIs) | Every top finance app (Mint, Simplifi, YNAB) has this. Gives the "big picture". | Medium |
| 8 | **Monthly Financial Report** — Auto-generated PDF/card summary: "June 2026 — You earned ₹X, spent ₹Y, saved ₹Z" | Users love shareable monthly snapshots. Can be tied to your existing Export page. | Medium |
| 9 | **Recurring Transaction Templates** — "Auto-log rent ₹15,000 on the 1st of every month" | Saves time for users with predictable bills (rent, EMI, salary). | Medium |
| 10 | **Expense Comparison** — "You spent 20% more on Groceries this month vs last month" | Month-over-month comparison insights are a top-requested feature. | Easy |

## 🎯 My Top 5 Recommendations (Best ROI for Your Project)

> These are ordered by **impact × ease of implementation**:

3. **Safe-to-Spend Card** — One KPI card addition to your dashboard, massive UX improvement
4. **Subscription Tracker** — Highly practical, auto-detects recurring transactions from your existing data
5. **Expense Comparison (Month vs Month)** — Simple analytics upgrade, huge insight value
